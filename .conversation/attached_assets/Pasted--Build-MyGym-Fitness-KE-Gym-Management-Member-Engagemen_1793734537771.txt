# Build MyGym Fitness KE — Gym Management & Member Engagement Platform

## 1. Project Overview

Build a production-oriented, responsive web application called **MyGym Fitness KE**.

This is a gym management and member engagement system designed for a single gym in Kenya.

The application must help gym staff manage members, subscriptions, payments, attendance, access restrictions, fitness assessments, workout plans, and events. Members should have a simple mobile-friendly portal for viewing their subscriptions, fitness progress, workout plans, and upcoming activities.

The gym currently manages attendance using a handwritten registration book. The new application should digitize its daily operations while remaining affordable, reliable, and extremely easy to use.

**Build a working full-stack application, not a static prototype or a collection of disconnected screens.**

### Core principles

- Keep the architecture simple and maintainable.
- Prioritize operational workflows over decorative dashboards.
- Use real database persistence and backend validation.
- Design for nontechnical gym owners and staff.
- Make all interfaces responsive and mobile-friendly.
- Use Kenyan shillings (KES), Kenyan phone numbers, and the Africa/Nairobi timezone.
- Avoid unnecessary external services and recurring costs.
- Do not implement unnecessary AI functionality.
- Do not introduce microservices, Redis, Celery, or complicated infrastructure without a demonstrated need.
- Do not store member profile photographs. Use initials-based avatars.
- Deliver features incrementally and verify them before proceeding.

The design direction has already been agreed upon.

Build a modern, minimal, light-themed gym management interface.

Visual characteristics:
- White and very light gray backgrounds
- Forest-green primary accents
- Dark charcoal text
- Clean typography and generous spacing
- Rounded but professional cards
- Clear status indicators
- Consistent outlined icons
- Minimal visual clutter
- Responsive desktop dashboards and mobile layouts

Use "MyGym Fitness KE" as the product name.

The gym's official branding already exists but may be supplied later. Keep brand assets and color tokens easily replaceable.

Use circular avatars displaying members' initials, such as JK for Jane Kamau.

Do not implement member photo uploads.

## 4. Authentication and User Roles

Implement four roles:

### Owner
Has full operational access, including financial reporting, gym settings, membership management, events, trainers, and staff administration.

### Receptionist
Can:
- Register and edit members
- Manage subscriptions
- Record membership payments
- Perform QR check-ins
- View attendance
- Handle permitted access overrides
- Assist members with events

Cannot view gym-wide revenue reports or change sensitive system settings.

### Trainer
Can:
- View assigned clients
- Record fitness assessments
- Manage exercises and workout templates
- Review questionnaire responses
- Recommend, approve, and adjust workout plans
- Track client progress

Cannot access financial reports or sensitive payment administration.

### Member
Can:
- View their subscription and expiry date
- Display their membership QR code
- View assigned workout plans
- View historical fitness progress
- Browse and book gym events
- Complete onboarding questionnaires

Members must never see another member's private information.

Implement secure staff authentication.

For members, use the registered phone number as the primary member identifier and a verified email address for delivering one-time login codes in the MVP.

The member must prove control of the email address associated with their account. Do not allow access simply by entering a phone number.

Implement OTP expiration, attempt limits, rate limiting, secure code storage, and session management.

Use an email-provider abstraction. During development, allow codes to be inspected in a clearly labeled development-only environment. Never expose login codes in production.

Provide an appropriate staff-assisted recovery process for members without access to their registered email.

## 5. Membership Management

The gym currently has two monthly membership tiers:

**Off-Peak**
- KES 4,000
- Access allowed from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM

**Peak Access**
- KES 5,000
- Exact permitted operating hours must be configurable and confirmed by the owner.

Do not assume the peak-access plan permits unlimited access until configured.

The gym owner must be able to:
- Create and edit membership plans
- Set monthly prices
- Configure operating hours
- Configure access windows by plan and day of week
- Activate or deactivate plans
- View subscription statistics

Memberships last one calendar month from the relevant start date, with correct handling of month-end dates and timezones.

Create a clear, configurable renewal policy and prevent overlapping subscription periods unless explicitly supported.

Each member should have:
- Unique membership number
- Full name
- Phone number
- Optional email initially, required before email OTP login
- Initials-based avatar
- Registration date
- Current subscription
- Subscription history
- Attendance history
- Payment history
- Fitness assessment history
- Assigned workout plan
- Membership status

Statuses should distinguish active, expired, cancelled, and other genuinely necessary conditions.

Implement member creation, editing, search, filtering, pagination, and subscription renewals.

Support CSV and Excel member imports, with:
- Column mapping
- Preview before import
- Field validation
- Duplicate detection
- Import error reporting
- Transaction-safe persistence

## 6. Payment Management

Members currently pay through M-Pesa, but payments will initially be verified and entered manually by staff.

Support:
- Recording payment amount
- M-Pesa transaction reference
- Payment date
- Member
- Subscription or event being paid for
- Staff member recording payment
- Payment history and corrections

Prevent accidental duplicate M-Pesa transaction references.

Do not treat manually entered payments as independently verified by Safaricom.

Use a clear distinction between staff-recorded payments and automatically verified payments.

Financial dashboards and gym-wide revenue reports must only be visible to the owner.

Prepare the payment domain to support future M-Pesa Daraja integration, but do not implement live transaction verification in the MVP.

## 7. QR-Based Attendance

Every member must have a unique digital membership card containing a scannable QR code.

The QR code must contain an unpredictable, revocable identifier, not personal data.

The member can:
- View the QR code in the portal
- Save or print their QR card

Reception staff can:
- Scan using a laptop webcam
- Scan using a smartphone camera
- Search manually by member name, phone, or membership number

Use browser camera APIs and a reliable QR scanning library.

Camera access must work in supported secure browser contexts.

### Check-in workflow

1. Receptionist scans QR or searches for the member.
2. Backend retrieves membership information.
3. Display member name, initials, membership type, and current status.
4. Validate current subscription and permitted access hours using server-side Africa/Nairobi time.
5. Display an appropriate success or restriction state.
6. Receptionist explicitly confirms the check-in.
7. Save attendance with timestamp and staff identity.

Scanning a QR code must not automatically record a visit.

### Access restriction handling

If a member:
- Arrives outside their permitted hours
- Has an expired subscription
- Has an inactive or invalid membership

Show a clear warning.

Allow authorized staff to approve an exception, provided they enter a reason.

Record:
- Member
- Restriction type
- Original restriction
- Staff member approving
- Explanation
- Timestamp

Access overrides must be auditable.

Prevent accidental duplicate check-ins within a configurable interval.

Provide attendance history, filters, and summary reporting.

QR codes are not biometric identity verification. Do not claim they prevent sharing between members.

## 8. Fitness Assessments

During registration or onboarding, staff and trainers should be able to capture:

- Weight in kilograms
- Height in centimeters
- BMI
- Visceral fat
- Body-fat percentage
- Muscle percentage

Calculate BMI automatically from weight and height.

Store every assessment historically.

Allow trainers to enter new assessments and compare results over time.

Provide progress charts and changes from previous assessments.

Support monthly reassessment reminders.

Ensure optional measurements can be left blank if equipment does not provide them.

Apply input validation and sensible units. Treat measurements as health-related information requiring appropriate privacy, consent, and access restrictions.

## 9. Member Fitness Questionnaire

New members should complete a mobile-friendly fitness questionnaire.

Questions should capture:
- Fitness experience: beginner, intermediate, advanced
- Primary fitness goals
- Days available per week
- Preferred training days
- Relevant physical limitations or injuries
- Training preferences

Store the questionnaire against the member profile.

Provide a secure, expiring onboarding link that does not expose other member records.

For the MVP, support copying or manually sending the questionnaire link.

The system should be designed to automatically deliver this link through WhatsApp later.

## 10. Workout Management

Trainers should manage an exercise library containing:
- Exercise name
- Description
- Muscle group
- Equipment
- Difficulty
- Sets and repetitions guidance
- Optional coaching instructions

Trainers should be able to create workout templates with:
- Template name
- Fitness objective
- Experience level
- Weekly training frequency
- Training days
- Ordered exercises
- Sets, repetitions, and rest periods

The system should recommend suitable templates using transparent rules based on the member's goals, experience level, and available training days.

Do not use a paid LLM or generative AI for initial recommendations.

Recommendations must be reviewed and approved by trainers.

The trainer should be able to customize the recommended plan before assignment.

Members should see:
- Current approved plan
- Weekly workout schedule
- Exercises
- Sets and repetitions
- Trainer notes
- Fitness progress

Members will not log individual workout sets or repetitions in the MVP.

## 11. Events and Activities

The gym owner should be able to schedule fitness-oriented activities such as:
- Hikes
- Bootcamps
- Fitness camps
- Group fitness activities
- Other gym community events

Provide a calendar and event management interface.

Each event should support:
- Name
- Description
- Date and time
- Location
- Capacity
- Free or paid status
- Price in KES, where applicable
- Registration deadline
- Booking status

Members should be able to open an event and reserve a spot using the member portal.

Prevent overbooking using backend transaction-safe capacity checks.

Support cancellation and appropriate capacity release.

For paid events, record M-Pesa payments manually.

Separate reservation status from payment status.

Provide member lists, payment status, and attendance information for each event.

Prepare the system to send event invitations through WhatsApp in a future release.

## 12. Notification and Engagement System

Build a provider-independent notification engine.

Initial notification types:

### Inactivity
If an active member has not checked in for 14 consecutive days, create a follow-up notification.

Do not repeatedly create duplicate reminders for the same inactivity episode.

### Membership Renewal
Generate reminders:
- Three days before expiry
- On the expiry date

Avoid sending an expiry reminder when a renewal has already been recorded.

### Onboarding
Generate welcome and questionnaire invitation messages for new members.

### Fitness Assessment
Generate monthly reassessment reminders.

### Events
Generate invitations and booking confirmations.

For the MVP:
- Store notifications in the database.
- Display pending notifications in a staff notification center.
- Allow authorized staff to copy message content or open an appropriate manual WhatsApp link.
- Record manual follow-up actions and outcomes.
- Do not claim a message was delivered simply because a link was opened.

Design an integration interface for future WhatsApp Business API sending.

When the provider is connected later, support delivery attempts, failures, retries, opt-in status, and appropriate message templates.

The notification engine must not depend on unreliable in-process FastAPI background tasks for scheduled delivery.

Use a persistent, idempotent job model and a separately triggered scheduled worker.

## 16. Quality and Acceptance Criteria

Do not consider a feature complete merely because its screen renders.

For each feature:
1. Implement the frontend interaction.
2. Implement the relevant backend endpoints.
3. Persist data to PostgreSQL.
4. Enforce authorization and validation.
5. Handle loading, empty, error, and success states.
6. Add appropriate tests.
7. Verify the complete user journey.

Required test scenarios include:

- An off-peak member checking in at 11 AM is permitted.
- The same member attempting access at 6 PM is flagged.
- A staff override requires a reason and creates an audit record.
- An expired membership triggers a warning.
- A duplicate QR scan does not create unintended attendance records.
- A membership renewal updates the effective subscription correctly.
- A receptionist cannot access owner-only revenue endpoints.
- A member cannot access another member's assessment or workout plan.
- A trainer can create and approve a workout plan.
- Event capacity cannot be exceeded by simultaneous bookings.
- An inactive member generates one appropriate follow-up task after 14 days.
- Renewal reminders are not duplicated or scheduled for already-renewed subscriptions.
